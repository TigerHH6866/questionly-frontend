The Platform ("Questionly") takes the importance of your personal information seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share and protect your personal information when you use the Platform's question OCR recognition, LLM parsing, question-bank management and paper assembly functions, and what rights you have.

> **Please read carefully:** The Platform mainly serves teachers. Before using it, please read and understand this Privacy Policy carefully — **in particular the provisions on transmitting question images and text to third-party LLM interfaces (Article 3), and your compliance obligations regarding any student personal information that may appear in question-bank content (Article 5)**.

## Article 1 — Personal information we collect

We follow the principles of lawfulness, legitimacy, necessity and good faith, and collect only the personal information needed to provide the service.

**1.1　Account registration and sign-in information**

When you register an account we collect your email address in order to create the account, verify your identity and let you sign in. If you choose to sign in with a third-party account, we obtain the unique identifier, avatar and nickname associated with that account to the extent you authorise it (as shown on the authorisation page).

**1.2　Information generated while using the service**

- (1) **Question images and text**: when you use recognition, the question images or documents you upload are transmitted to our servers for processing; when you use LLM parsing, analysis or generation, the related question text is transmitted to third-party AI model interfaces. Please note in particular: the images and text you upload must not contain any personal information that identifies a specific student, such as names, student numbers, ID card numbers, marks or home addresses (see Article 5).
- (2) **Operation logs**: to keep the service stable and secure we automatically collect your IP address, browser type, operating system version, access times and operation records.

**1.3　Device information**

When you visit the Platform we may collect your device model, operating system version and screen resolution, to adapt page display and diagnose technical faults.

**1.4　Information we do not collect**

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photo library (other than question images or documents you actively choose to upload), or any other personal information unrelated to the question-bank service. We do not require you to provide unnecessary personal information in order to use the basic functions.

## Article 2 — How we use your personal information

**2.1** To create and manage your account, verify your identity and keep the account secure.

**2.2** To provide the core service: running OCR text extraction on the question images you upload, and transmitting question text to LLM interfaces for structured parsing, answer generation or format tidying.

**2.3** To keep the service secure and stable: analysing operation logs for abnormal access, preventing network attacks and tracing security incidents.

**2.4** To improve the service: after de-identification or anonymisation, analysing feature usage frequency and recognition accuracy trends to improve the product. We do not use information that identifies you for unrelated purposes.

## Article 3 — Question data and third-party AI services (core provision)

**3.1　Why data is transmitted**

The Platform's OCR and LLM functions rely on third-party technical services. To recognise and parse questions, the images or text you upload must be transmitted to our servers, and we in turn call the interfaces of the third-party AI models we work with.

**3.2　Restrictions we place on third parties**

We have signed data processing agreements with our third-party AI providers requiring them to:

- (1) use your question data only to fulfil the current recognition or parsing request, and not to retain or copy it or use it to train their models;
- (2) apply the technical and organisational measures needed to keep the data secure in transit and in processing;
- (3) delete the relevant data promptly once the request is fulfilled.

**3.3　Your choice**

If you do not want your question data transmitted to third-party AI interfaces, you may use only manual entry and not use recognition or LLM parsing. Question data entered manually is stored only on the Platform's servers and does not involve third-party AI processing.

**3.4　Sensitive personal information**

Please never upload images or text containing a student's ID card number, student registration number, home address, contact details, biometric information, medical or health information, or anything whose leakage could endanger a student's personal or financial safety. The Platform does not request such information; the risk of leakage arising from your own upload is borne by you.

## Article 4 — Storage and protection

**4.1　Where data is stored**

Your personal information and question-bank data are stored on servers within the territory of the People's Republic of China. We do not transfer your personal information abroad.

**4.2　How long data is kept**

- (1) **Account information**: kept for as long as your account exists. After you close your account we will delete or anonymise your account information and question-bank data within 15 working days, unless laws or regulations require otherwise.
- (2) **Operation logs**: to safeguard network security we keep operation logs for no less than six months, in line with the requirements of the Cybersecurity Law.

**4.3　Security measures**

We protect your information with encrypted transmission (HTTPS/TLS), access control and data backup. Although we make every effort to keep it secure, no internet environment is absolutely safe; please understand this and back up important question-bank data locally yourself.

## Article 5 — Your compliance obligations regarding personal information in question-bank content (particularly important)

**5.1** As a teacher, the question content you upload (exam papers, exercises) may incidentally contain student names, student numbers or mark rankings. You undertake that, before uploading any material containing student personal information to the Platform, you have already de-identified it (for example by masking names or removing student numbers), or have obtained the separate consent of the student's guardian (where applicable).

**5.2** The Platform accepts no legal liability arising from student personal information contained in content you upload. If your breach of this Article causes a third party (including a student or their guardian) to make a claim against the Platform, you must handle it and compensate the Platform for all losses it suffers as a result.

**5.3** If we discover that content you uploaded contains obvious personal information of a minor that has not been de-identified, we may delete the content immediately and suspend your account as appropriate.

## Article 6 — Sharing, transfer and public disclosure

**6.1** We do not sell your personal information.

**6.2** We do not share your personal information with third parties except in the following cases:

- (1) with your explicit consent;
- (2) where necessary to provide the AI services described in Article 3, by transmitting question data to contracted third-party AI providers;
- (3) where required by laws or regulations, or lawfully requested by a competent authority;
- (4) to protect the lawful rights of the Platform or the public (for example if you use the Platform for unlawful activity).

**6.3** If the Platform is merged, divided or transfers assets, your personal information may be transferred as part of those assets. We will require the recipient to remain bound by this Policy and will notify you separately.

## Article 7 — Your rights

Under the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China you have the following rights over your personal information:

**7.1　Access and copy**: you can view your account information on the account settings page and export your question-bank data.

**7.2　Correct and complete**: you may correct inaccurate account information (such as changing the linked email address).

**7.3　Delete**: you may delete any question in your bank, and you may request that your account be closed; after closure we will delete or anonymise your information as described in Article 4.

**7.4　Withdraw consent**: for information processed on the basis of consent (such as third-party sign-in authorisation) you may withdraw that consent. Withdrawal does not affect the validity of processing carried out before it.

**7.5　Responding to your requests**: you may exercise these rights using the contact details at the end of this Policy. We will respond within 15 working days; to keep you safe we may first need to verify your identity.

## Article 8 — Protection of minors

**8.1** The Platform does not offer registration to minors. We require registered users to be adults (teachers) aged 18 or over.

**8.2** If we discover that we have collected a minor's personal information without verifiable prior guardian consent (for example, where uploaded question content contains un-de-identified student information), we will delete the relevant data as soon as possible. If you are a guardian and become aware of such a case, please contact us.

**9.2** You can view the latest version of this Policy at any time in the footer of the Platform's home page or in account settings.
